How Long Would America's Oil Reserve Last?

The U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) is an emergency stockpile. If it were the nation's only source of oil, here's how fast the hourglass would empty.

~20 days
if the SPR alone had to fuel the entire United States.

About 398 million barrels in the reserve ÷ roughly 20 million barrels consumed nationwide every day ≈ 20 days of total supply. The SPR is never used this way — it tops up the market during disruptions — but it shows the scale.
